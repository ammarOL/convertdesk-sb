# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

ConvertDesk is for small teams, founders, service businesses, and support-led SaaS operators who want a website assistant that answers from their own files and captures qualified leads without a custom AI implementation. They are usually trying to turn existing FAQs, docs, and company information into a deployable customer-facing chatbot.

## Product Purpose

ConvertDesk lets users create RAG-powered chatbots, upload a knowledge base, configure bot behavior and appearance, embed the assistant on a website, and collect lead details through the chat flow. Success means a visitor gets a relevant answer grounded in the business's own content, and the business gets a useful conversation or lead instead of a missed inquiry.

## Brand Personality

Precise, hospitable, and operational. The brand should feel like a capable front desk: fast to set up, calm under pressure, and tuned to the business it represents.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI chatbot hype, vague "automate everything" claims, pastel template SaaS pages, developer-starter boilerplate, and promises for integrations or channels that are not visible in the current app. Do not make the landing page feel like a Supabase starter or a dashboard documentation page.

## Design Principles

- Show the deployed assistant, not abstract AI.
- Sell the workflow: upload knowledge, tune behavior, match the brand, deploy.
- Ground claims in available product features.
- Make lead capture feel like part of service, not a pop-up trap.
- Keep the page confident enough for business buyers and simple enough for a first-time AI user.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for marketing copy and controls. Keep motion optional through reduced-motion preferences, avoid color-only meaning, and make the primary conversion path reachable through clear semantic links.
